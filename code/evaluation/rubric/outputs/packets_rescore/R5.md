### Summary

Store 1064 is forecast at approximately 45,634 units for the week of 2015-05-25, which sits below the recent 4-, 8- and 12-week averages of roughly 57,000-59,000 units that the model draws on as its strongest upward influences. The main downward influences in the model are the absence of recorded promotion activity, a five-day open-days value, and time since the last promotion. Together these suggest the week is being modelled as a shorter, non-promotional trading period rather than a change in underlying demand, so it may be worth confirming the trading calendar and promotion schedule are correctly recorded, and reviewing whether staffing cover and store-level replenishment built on recent averages still fit this week.
